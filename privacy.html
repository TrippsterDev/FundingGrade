Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

Funding Grade is a free educational site. This page explains what happens to information when you use it, in plain English. The short version: we never ask for your name, email or phone number, we don't sell data, and the site uses Google Analytics and Google ads like most free sites do.

The loan readiness estimator

The estimator asks about your business: revenue range, time in business, credit score range, debt, industry, loan amount and purpose. In Advanced mode you can also enter EBITDA or net income, monthly revenue and monthly debt payments. It never asks for your name, contact details, Social Security number or account numbers, and it does not pull your credit.

Your score is calculated in your browser. To write your plain-English explanation, the site sends your score, the factor breakdown and the figures above to our AI provider, Anthropic, through our server. That information is used only to generate your explanation. We don't save your answers or your results, and we can't connect them to you.

Server logs

Our hosting provider, Netlify, keeps standard technical logs (such as IP address, browser type and the page requested) to run and secure the site. We don't use these logs to identify visitors.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand which pages are read and how visitors find the site. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technology to collect information such as pages visited, approximate location, device and browser. We see this only in aggregate. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Advertising

We show ads through Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info. For more on how Google uses data, see How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Partner links

Some links in your results and on this site go to lending partners. If you click one, you leave Funding Grade, and that partner's own privacy policy applies. Some partners pay us a commission if you apply. That never changes your score. Partners who pay us may be listed first among the partner links.

Docs That Close, the template shop linked on this site, is run by the same team as Funding Grade. It is our own shop, not an affiliate.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent personal information.
  • We don't ask for or store your name, email, phone number, Social Security number or account numbers.
  • We don't pull your credit or share your answers with lenders.

Children

Funding Grade is meant for business owners and is not directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, use the opt-out links above, or browse in a private window. The estimator works without cookies.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the site handles information, we'll update this page and the effective date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@fundinggrade.com.
